A

Engagement overview

Prepared: HostReviewed: ____

Objective. Larkspur Baking Co. sells soft pretzels, snack cakes and holiday gift boxes to grocers and cafes nationwide. The Controller's FY2025 statements show a net loss on record sales. The CEO is not an accountant and wants an independent team to review the statements before they go to the bank. Find the most important problems, measure their effect on the statements, and recommend fixes.

Start here

  1. 01Download the PBC documents (D). Everyone needs the Comparative Financial Statements workbook.
  2. 02Assign the engagement team (A.2) so each person owns one area.
  3. 03Run the planning analytics (B) before opening the detail records. Every flagged line becomes a question.
  4. 04Perform the procedures (C) in the Nov–Dec records, then quantify each finding.

You won't finish every procedure. Judges reward the biggest, best-explained findings, not the longest list.

B

Planning analytics: methodology

Prepared: HostReviewed: ____

Purpose. Analytical procedures tell auditors where to look. Compare the numbers to what they should be; anything that doesn't fit becomes a question for the detail records. Each analytic follows four steps: expect (decide what the number should be), compare (actual vs expectation and prior year), investigate (find the cause in the records, not by asking management to guess), conclude (business explanation, or a misstatement you can measure).

C-1Gross margin fell almost 5 pointsInventory and COGSExistence, ValuationRisk: High
Analytic signal
Same products and co-packers, yet margin dropped from 47.2% to 42.6%. Inventory went to $0.
Perform in
Inventory Count tab, General Journal (12/31 entries), Trial Balance; PBC-08
Conclusion
Team to document in the Findings Log
Reviewer note: need a nudge?

Larkspur uses a periodic system: COGS = beginning inventory + purchases − ending inventory. Was every part of that entry recorded?

C-2December sales and receivables jumpedRevenueOccurrence, CutoffRisk: High
Analytic signal
December was almost 2× an average month, and pretzels and cakes rose too, not only gift boxes. Receivables grew faster than sales.
Perform in
Sales Journal vs Shipping Log; sales journal month totals; PBC-06
Conclusion
Team to document in the Findings Log
Reviewer note: need a nudge?

Revenue is recorded when goods ship (FOB shipping point). Compare invoice dates with ship dates for the last days of December. Check invoice numbers for repeats and re-add the monthly totals.

Reporting: presentation to the panel

5–7 minutes

One PowerPoint delivered to a panel that includes practicing auditors from Forvis Mazars. Presenters who run past 7 minutes may be cut off.

SlideSectionContent
1HeadlineWho you are, what you reviewed, and your conclusion: can the bank rely on these statements?

4Effect on the statementsAt least one correcting journal entry. Net income, gross margin and inventory before and after.

Discussion questions to answer in your presentation

  1. Which analytic pointed you to your biggest finding, and what did you expect to see?
  2. After your corrections, does Larkspur look like a profitable, growing company? Show the numbers.
  3. Which flagged changes turned out to have a normal business reason?
  4. What single control change would have prevented the most problems?

Excel techniques for data tests

  • Re-foot: =SUM(range) vs the posted total.
  • Duplicates: =COUNTIF(B:B,B6)>1 beside each invoice number.
  • Gaps: sort by number; subtract each from the next. Anything but 1 is a gap.
  • Blanks and limits: Data › Filter, then blanks or "between 4,500 and 4,999".
  • Transposition: a difference divisible by 9 often means swapped digits.

Engagement rules

  • Use only the case files. Don't share findings with other teams.
  • Judges and the host answer questions about logistics and case facts, not whether something is an error.
  • All names, companies and numbers are fictional. Don't contact any real business.
  • Report facts and risks. Accountants don't conclude intent; don't accuse anyone of wrongdoing.
